# Lead-time analysis

No defensible lead-time distribution can be reported yet.

The current corpus contains planning and permit dates, but no candidate has both a credibly
sourced confirmed opening date and an earlier source timeline in the database. Search snippets,
assumed seasonal openings, and current directory presence are not sufficient.

Current sample size: **0**. Median, P25, P75, minimum, and maximum are therefore not available.

The next backfill step is to verify opening dates for a bounded subset of Salem permit candidates
using official company location pages or dated company announcements, then calculate:

1. first source signal to confirmed opening; and
2. first actionable signal to confirmed opening.

Until then, Opportunity Intel has not proven that its leads arrive early enough.
