# Phase 0.9 source lead-time analysis

Run date: 2026-08-19

| Source/signal | Observed lifecycle position | Strength | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planning/zoning | Early | Exact project and address | Often owner-only; 20/74 land-use-only records actionable and just 30% of those contactable |
| Building permit | Mid-stage | Concrete work and useful cleaning timing | Current corpus is dominated by historical permits, so live yield is not yet measured |
| Sign permit | Late-mid | Often exposes operator identity | Too few independent examples for a rate |
| Official company/location | Variable | Strong operator and contact evidence | Often appears after the municipal signal |
| Hiring | Pre-opening | Good operator/timing evidence | No adequate current sample |
| Occupancy | Very late | High certainty | Historical occupancy-only sample produced no current actionable leads |

The strongest observed combination is municipal land-use plus official-company evidence: 4/4 actionable, contactable, and A/B-ready. Municipal land-use plus an official public directory or registry is also 1/1 in each case. These samples are small and establish a useful hypothesis, not statistical certainty.

Planning alone supplies volume but weak conversion: 74 samples, 20 actionable (27.0%); among those actionables, 30.0% contactable and 20.0% A/B-ready. The practical source strategy is therefore municipal discovery followed by operator/company corroboration, not municipal discovery alone.
