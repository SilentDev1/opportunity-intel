# Vendor validation plan

Recruit three to five New Hampshire vendors across commercial cleaning, IT/MSP, security/access
control, commercial insurance, and pest control. No outreach has been performed yet.

For each tester:

1. record services, service territory, preferred industries, minimum account size, and excluded
   business types;
2. generate 5–10 reviewed opportunities from `phase05-actionable-matrix.csv`;
3. provide business/location, stage, plain-language “why now,” likely need with reason, and official
   provenance—never an unsupported claim that the business definitely needs the service;
4. ask the tester to label each useful/maybe/not useful, already known/not known, early enough/too
   late, would contact/would not contact, and missing information;
5. optionally track contact attempted, conversation, quote/meeting, customer won, no response, or
   bad lead.

Success criteria for the next gate: at least three testers complete ratings, a majority of served
leads are useful or maybe, at least 30% were previously unknown, and vendors say HIGH/MEDIUM timing
is early enough. Outcomes must be recorded as observations, not testimonials inferred by the team.

The vendor-specific export must contain: business, city, industry, stage, why it matches, why now,
evidence summary, opening window if sourced, legitimate public contact route, and source URL. Raw
permit jargon remains available behind the evidence link but is not the lead headline.
