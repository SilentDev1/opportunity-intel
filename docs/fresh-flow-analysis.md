## Highest-value missing current feeds

1. Current issued building permits, because they can identify concrete buildout timing closer to cleaning-vendor selection.
2. Current sign permits, because they can expose the operating business while work is active.
3. Current certificates of occupancy/inspection activity, as a late-stage confirmation and update trigger.

Current planning intake is already represented in several municipalities. Business registrations are lower priority because they usually lack a usable exact opening/buildout event. No new collector was added without first confirming a durable, automatable public endpoint.
