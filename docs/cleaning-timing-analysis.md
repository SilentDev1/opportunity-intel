# Phase 0.9 commercial-cleaning timing analysis

Run date: 2026-08-19

The cleaning model produces nine testable leads: one CLEANING_A and eight CLEANING_B. Their stored stages are one PRE_OPENING, one LOCATION_CONFIRMED, and seven PLANNING.

The single strongest record is Wonder at PRE_OPENING: named operator, known site, useful company contact, and municipal/company corroboration. Bluebird Salem is LOCATION_CONFIRMED and is the only fresh cleaning A/B lead observed in the incomplete week. The seven planning-stage B leads are usable because operator and contact routes are known, but their timing is earlier and less certain.

The best apparent trigger is:

> operator confirmed + location confirmed + active buildout or pre-opening evidence

That balances a real contact route with a plausible vendor-selection window. Planning-only should normally be a monitor event; it becomes a B alert only when operator identity and verified contactability offset the timing uncertainty. Occupancy is likely too late for first outreach, but the current corpus has no adequate live occupancy sample to prove that.

No opening-date interval is estimated because historical timestamps are incomplete and the sample does not support a defensible day-count model.
